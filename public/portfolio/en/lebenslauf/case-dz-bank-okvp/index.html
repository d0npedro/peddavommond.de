Starting point

DZ BANK / adesso, 2020. Fiducia & GAD sales platforms were to move onto the new SDK/microservice platform OKVP.

Cut

Decoupling into independently shippable “Features”; multi-stage OpenShift pipelines; observability with Prometheus, Grafana and Dynatrace.

Implementation

OKVP splits grown platforms into features. Delivery via OpenShift; service discovery via Consul.

Evidence

No AI/LLM component. Assessed via independently shippable features, pipelines and observability.

Result

Decoupled features, controlled delivery. No published performance metrics.
